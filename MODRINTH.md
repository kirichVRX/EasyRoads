# 🛣️ EasyRoads

> **Effortless, organic, and realistic 3D road building for Minecraft.**

> ⚠️ **Note: EasyRoads is currently in Alpha Testing!** Features and mechanics are actively being refined. Feel free to test and share your feedback/bug reports.

Tired of building roads block-by-block? Sick of rigid 90-degree turns and awkward stairs on hills? **EasyRoads** introduces procedural 3D curve building into Minecraft, allowing you to lay down smooth highways, urban avenues, and winding countryside paths in seconds!

---

## ✨ Features at a Glance

* 📐 **Smooth 3D Bézier Curves** — Roads adapt naturally to rolling hills, mountain passes, and gentle turns with mathematically smooth transitions.
* 🛠️ **In-Game Interactive Editor** — Enter edit mode with a single keypress (`F7`), drop anchor points with your mouse, and watch the road outline calculate in real time.
* 🎛️ **3D Transformation Gizmos** — Adjust elevation (`Mouse Wheel`) and fine-tune coordinates (`Arrow keys / WASD`) directly in 3D space.
* 🎨 **Road Preset Studio** — Configure custom road cross-sections, multi-layer profiles (asphalt, gravel sub-base, curbs, sidewalks, and lane markings).
* ↩️ **Full Undo Support** — Placed a road segment by accident? `/road undo` safely rolls back block changes and restores original terrain.
* ⚡ **Performance Friendly** — Fast voxelization designed to run cleanly on servers without causing lag spikes.

---

## 🎮 How to Use

1. Press **`F7`** to enter **Road Edit Mode**.
2. Select the **Add Point** tool from the on-screen overlay.
3. **Left-click** along the terrain to place your road's path nodes.
4. Switch to **Adjust Tool** to tweak the height (`Mouse Wheel`) or direction if needed.
5. Press **`Caps Lock`** to open the **Preset Studio** and choose your road materials and width.
6. Press **`Enter`** — your road will instantly be built into the world!

## 💬 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

<details>
<summary><b>Can I use this in my modpack?</b></summary>
Yes! You are completely free to include EasyRoads in any public or private modpack.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does it work on multiplayer servers?</b></summary>
Yes, EasyRoads is designed with client-server architecture in mind. The mod must be installed on both the server and the client.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I change the keybindings?</b></summary>
Yes! All keybinds can be reconfigured in standard Minecraft settings: <i>Options -> Controls -> Key Binds -> Road Builder</i>.
</details>
