# 🛣️ EasyRoads

> **The ultimate road-building tool for Minecraft city planners and builders. Design and construct smooth highways, city streets, and winding paths in seconds!**

Tired of manually laying down thousands of asphalt blocks or struggling with awkward 90-degree stairs on hills? **EasyRoads** is built specifically for city builders, map makers, and infrastructure enthusiasts to supercharge construction speed by up to **10x**.

Plan your road network with organic **3D Bézier curves**, preview cross-sections with live 3D gizmos, and generate entire roads with one click.

---

## ⚡ Why EasyRoads?

* 🏙️ **Built for City Builders** — Effortlessly connect distant cities, build realistic multi-lane highways, interchanges, and mountain serpentines.
* 📈 **10x Faster Construction** — Place a few anchor points, adjust curve slopes, and let the mod voxelize the road instantly. No more repetitive block placing!
* 📐 **Smooth 3D Curves** — Mathematically clean transitions without ugly jagged edges or sharp elevation steps.
* 🎛️ **Visual 3D Editor (`F7`)** — Drop anchor nodes, adjust heights with `Mouse Wheel`, and nudge positions via `Arrow Keys / WASD`.
* 🎨 **Preset Studio (`Caps Lock`)** — Switch between road styles (asphalt, curbs, sidewalks, and lane markings) or build your own custom profiles.
* ↩️ **Safe Undo (`/road undo`)** — Made a mistake? Instantly roll back placed segments and restore original terrain.

---

## 🎮 Quick Start in 4 Steps

1. Press **`F7`** to open the **Road Editor**.
2. **Left-click** along the terrain to drop road anchor points.
3. Press **`Caps Lock`** to choose your road style & materials in the **Preset Studio**.
4. Press **`Enter`** — your complete road is generated into the world!

---

## ⌨️ Controls & Keybinds

| Action | Default Key | Description |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Toggle Editor** | `F7` | Enter or exit road construction mode |
| **Preset Studio** | `Caps Lock` | Select road styles, width, and materials |
| **Build Road** | `Enter` | Instantly generate blocks along the curve |
| **Elevation ($Y$)** | `Mouse Wheel` | Raise or lower nodes in Adjust tool |
| **Fine-tune ($XZ$)** | `Arrow Keys` / `WASD` | Move nodes horizontally |
